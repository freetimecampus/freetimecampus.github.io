Privacy Policy

FreeTime is a free app for students that shows which friends are free between classes and helps students meet each other on campus. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. For students in Thailand, it follows Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019), known as the PDPA. Students in other countries are covered by the data protection law of their own country, where it applies. Last updated: November 17, 2026.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a password (stored only in protected form by our sign-in provider), your name and your nickname.
  • Profile: an optional profile photo; boy or girl (if you choose); your university and campus, and the campus time zone; and, if you add them, your school, major, minor, year and semester; and your personality type result if you take the quiz.
  • Timetable: the lessons you save (course name or code, section, teacher name as printed, day, time, room, and seminar dates), lessons you mark as cancelled, and private calendar events you add.
  • Friends and chats: your friend list, friend requests, mute and "don't show as free" settings, the messages you send (text, photos, videos, files and voice messages), likes, mentions, read marks and chat themes.
  • Photos, videos, files and voice messages in chats: they are stored privately on our server (files up to 20 MB, videos up to 50 MB, voice messages up to 2 minutes). Only the people in that chat can open them, through a temporary link that stops working after an hour.
  • Meet: your Friend crush picks, your Open table requests and the table chats you join.
  • Campus confessions: the notes and replies you write.
  • Notifications: a device token, so the app can send you alerts such as a friend being free, a new message or a class being cancelled. These alerts (except chat messages) are also kept in a list inside the app (the bell at the top of the Schedule page), so you can see ones you missed.
  • Calls: who called whom, when, and how long the call lasted (your call history). Voice and video calls go directly between the phones, are encrypted, and are never recorded or stored.
  • App usage and errors: how many times and at what times of day the app is opened, which tabs are used and for how long, and whether the timetable window was closed without adding a timetable (seen only by the FreeTime team, to improve the app), and error reports when something breaks in the app (the error, the screen and basic device information).

Why we may use your information (legal basis)

  • To give you the app you signed up for (performing our agreement with you): your account, timetable, friends, chats and the features that show when friends are free.
  • With your consent, for things you choose to turn on: your profile photo, camera, microphone, photo gallery and notifications. You can turn each of these off at any time in the app or in your phone settings.
  • For our legitimate interests: keeping FreeTime safe (reports, blocking, preventing abuse), fixing errors and improving the app with usage numbers.
  • When the law requires it.

Camera and microphone

FreeTime uses the camera only when you choose to scan a QR code, take a photo of your timetable, take a photo or video to send in a chat, or turn on your camera in a video call. It uses the microphone only while you record a voice message or video, or while you are in a call. FreeTime does not use your GPS location or your contacts.

Your photo gallery

If you allow it, the chat photo button shows your newest photos so you can pick one quickly. This happens only on your phone: FreeTime uploads only the photo you choose to send, and never your other photos. You can say no and still choose photos with "All photos".

Timetable photos

When you upload a photo or file of your timetable, it is sent through our server to Google's Gemini service so it can be read into a list of lessons. We do not keep the photo. Google processes it under its own terms.

Student ID and exam neighbors

If your timetable screenshot shows your student ID (for example at the top of AU Spark), FreeTime keeps it so it can tell you who will probably sit next to you in your final exams, because exam seats often go by student ID order. It is also used to fill in your year and semester of study. Your student ID is stored securely and is never shown to any other student or in the app. A week before the final exams, the student whose ID comes just before or just after yours in the same course may see your name and that course, and you see theirs. You can turn this off in Settings (Exam neighbors): your student ID is then deleted and not saved again.

Who can see your information

  • Your friends can see your lessons, times and rooms. You can hide the details (course names and rooms) in Settings with the Privacy option. Students who are not your friends cannot see your timetable.
  • Students on your campus can find you by name or nickname and may see you in "Suggested for you" and in Friend crush with your name, photo and campus. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Signed-in students can see your school, major, minor, year and semester on your profile, if you added them.
  • When FreeTime suggests you to another student on your campus, they can also see the friends you have in common and the classes you both take (only those shared classes, never your whole timetable). This is not shown if you hid your details or blocked them.
  • In one-to-one chats, only you and the friend you are chatting with can read your messages.
  • Boy or girl is shown only to the students at the same Open table.
  • Friend crush picks are never shown to anyone. Only a mutual pick is revealed, and then you both become friends.
  • Confessions are anonymous to other students. To keep the page safe, the FreeTime team can see who wrote a note or reply, and can remove it or block the writer.
  • To help students whose timetable did not upload fully, the FreeTime team can see a student's timetable for support and quality checks. The team never shares it with other students.
  • We do not sell your information. FreeTime has no ads today; if that ever changes, we will update this policy first and tell you in the app.

Class chats, group chats and safety

Every class has a group chat for the students who study it (same course, room and start time). Students in a class chat, group chat or table chat can see each other's nickname, profile photo and the messages written there, but not each other's timetable unless they are friends.

  • You can report any message and block any student. Blocked students' messages are hidden from you. You can unblock them in Settings.
  • You can delete your own messages.
  • Reports are reviewed by the FreeTime team. A report contains the message, who wrote it and who reported it.
  • When a student reports that a class is cancelled, classmates are asked to confirm it, and the report shows the reporter's nickname. Friends who are free at that time may be told that the class is cancelled.

Services we use

  • Supabase: stores the app's data and handles sign-in (servers in Singapore).
  • Google Gemini: reads timetable photos and files.
  • Expo and Google Firebase: deliver push notifications and app updates.
  • Sentry: receives error reports so we can fix problems (servers in the United States). It is set up not to collect personal details such as your name or email.

Storing your data outside Thailand

Your data is stored on Supabase servers in Singapore. Timetable photos are read by Google Gemini, notifications are delivered by Expo and Google Firebase, and error reports go to Sentry in the United States. We only use providers that protect personal data under their own data protection terms, and we send them only what each service needs.

How we protect it

Every connection to FreeTime is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and stored data is encrypted (AES-256). Database rules let each student open only their own data and what their friends share with them. Your student ID is never shown in the app. If a data breach ever puts your information at risk, we will inform the authorities and you as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. You can remove lessons, friends, your photo and your study information inside the app at any time.

  • The alerts in the bell list are deleted after 30 days. Open table requests are deleted after 2 days.
  • "Free now" group chats are deleted shortly after the shared free time ends.
  • To keep the service free, we may automatically delete the oldest chat photos, files and voice messages when storage runs low. They then show as "no longer available".

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Settings → Delete account → type DELETE to confirm. You can also email freetime.app.help@gmail.com from your registered address and we will delete it for you.

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, student ID, timetable, study information, friendships, messages, photos, files and voice messages, Meet data, notification tokens, the bell list and your login. This cannot be undone.

Your rights

In Thailand the PDPA gives you these rights, and many other countries give similar ones. You can: ask for a copy of your data; ask us to correct it; ask us to delete it (or delete your account yourself in the app); object to or ask us to limit how we use it; take your data to another service; and withdraw any consent you gave, at any time. To use these rights, email freetime.app.help@gmail.com. We will answer within 30 days. Students in Thailand can also complain to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC); students elsewhere can contact their local data protection authority.

Children

FreeTime is meant for students. It is not intended for children under 13.
